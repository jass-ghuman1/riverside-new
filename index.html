How We Think

Our Holistic
Approach

By focusing on the development of social, emotional, fine motor, gross motor, and cognitive skills, our carefully planned programs provide a well-rounded approach to early childhood development.

Hover or tap a star — every experience connects

Emotional Well-being

We prioritize creating a nurturing and supportive environment where children feel safe, loved, and valued. Our trained staff understands the importance of emotional connection, helping them develop positive self-image and emotional resilience.

Cognitive Development

We offer age-appropriate activities that stimulate thinking and problem-solving skills. Our teachers provide opportunities for exploration, discovery, and critical thinking, fostering intellectual curiosity.

Creative Expression

We value the power of creativity and encourage children to express themselves through drawing, painting, music, dance, and imaginative play, fostering their imagination and cognitive abilities.

Social Skills

We promote positive social interactions, helping children develop skills like sharing, taking turns, and empathy. Through group activities and collaborative play, children learn to communicate effectively and build relationships.

Physical Abilities

Our daycare provides ample opportunities for active play both indoors and outdoors. We offer activities like sports, games, dancing, and yoga, promoting physical coordination and overall well-being.

Our Philosophy

Why Natural
Materials?

We prioritize sensory experiences that engage children and promote exploration, creativity, and curiosity. Children will have opportunities to play in the snow, explore water, play in mud, sand and more.

Sustainability

Natural materials are biodegradable and sourced from renewable resources — a more environmentally-friendly option than plastic.

Development

Wooden toys promote fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and dexterity through precise, coordinated play.

Sensory Experience

Natural materials provide rich sensory experiences and a calming effect for children with anxiety or sensory processing differences.

Safety

Wooden toys are typically made from non-toxic materials, ensuring the safety and health of every child in our care.

Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

We are happy to answer! Check out our most common questions below.

What are your hours?

Our centre is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 am – 6:00 pm. The infant nursery is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 am – 5:30 pm. We are closed on all statutory holidays and Christmas break.

Where are you located?

We are located in the heart of the Riverside community in St. Albert at 10 Rose Gate #600, St. Albert, AB T8N 7N6 — just 1 minute from Ray Gibbons.

What age range do you accept?

At Riverside Daycare & OSC, we accept children between the ages of 12 months and 12 years across our six different programs.

What programs do you follow?

In alignment with the Alberta childcare curriculum, we take a holistic approach and implement the Reggio Emilia method. This focuses on emotional well-being, cognitive development, creative expression, social skills, and physical abilities. Learn more about Reggio Emilia.

Do you accept subsidy?

Yes, we accept subsidy approved prior to your child's enrollment provided by the Government of Alberta. Our centre is also approved for grant funding by the Canada-Alberta Early Learning and Child Care Agreement. Visit the Government of Alberta website for more information.

Do you provide snacks and meals?

Yes! We provide morning breakfast and afternoon snacks prepared in accordance with AHS food safety guidelines using nutritious, quality ingredients. Lunch is available as an optional supplemental program and is sourced daily from an AHS-approved and certified commercial kitchen. Menus are carefully planned to support balanced nutrition and accommodate allergies and dietary needs whenever possible.

What is your illness policy?

We follow the guidelines set out by Alberta Health. A child is not to be in attendance if they have any illness or flu-like symptoms. This ensures the safety of all children and staff. Full guidelines are outlined in the parents handbook provided upon enrolment.

What amenities does your centre have?

We offer dedicated indoor and outdoor play areas with sports, games, dancing, and yoga. Our OSC program provides spaces for homework completion, quiet reading, group games, and outdoor play, catering to children's interests and promoting social interaction.
